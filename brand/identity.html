PlanSphere

Plan Infinity and Travel Beyond the World

The idea

Three decisions, no ornament

The name gives a sphere and the slogan gives infinity. Drawn together honestly, they stop being two symbols and become one object: an endless route around a whole world.

A planet, not a map

Meridians and latitudes, lit from the upper left. A flat map would tie PlanSphere to one region. A sphere says the whole world is in scope — which is what the slogan promises.

A route that is also infinity

The orbit traces a lemniscate. It passes behind the planet on the left and across its face on the right, so the figure-eight reads as a real path around a real world rather than an ∞ glued onto a globe.

One warm point

Where the route crosses itself sits a single amber node: the trip being planned right now. It is the only warm colour in the system, so it is always the first thing the eye lands on.

Construction

Built on a 16-unit grid

The mark is drawn in a 128-unit square. Every measure below is a multiple of that grid, so the logo stays consistent whether it is embroidered on a cap or rendered at 20px in a nav bar.

  • Clear space, all sidesx = ¼ of mark height
  • Minimum size, mark20 px / 6 mm
  • Minimum size, horizontal lockup96 px / 25 mm
  • Orbit tilt−16°
  • Sphere diameter60 of 128 units
  • Node diameter10.8 units

One colour

It has to survive a rubber stamp

Gradients are a luxury. The single-colour cut keeps the over-and-under weave by knocking the front strand out of the sphere, so the orbit still reads in one ink.

Colour

Typography

Wayfinding, not marketing

Archivo comes out of signage lettering, which is exactly the voice an itinerary wants: legible at a glance, sturdy when set large and tight. Plex Mono handles everything with a time or a code attached.
